Normal 0 false false false EN-US X-NONE X-NONE /* Style Definitions */ table.MsoNormalTable {mso-style-name:"Table Normal"; mso-tstyle-rowband-size:0; mso-tstyle-colband-size:0; mso-style-noshow:yes; mso-style-priority:99; mso-style-parent:""; mso-padding-alt:0in 5.4pt 0in 5.4pt; mso-para-margin-top:0in; mso-para-margin-right:0in; mso-para-margin-bottom:8.0pt; mso-para-margin-left:0in; line-height:107%; mso-pagination:widow-orphan; font-size:11.0pt; font-family:"Calibri",sans-serif; mso-ascii-font-family:Calibri; mso-ascii-theme-font:minor-latin; mso-hansi-font-family:Calibri; mso-hansi-theme-font:minor-latin; mso-bidi-font-family:"Times New Roman"; mso-bidi-theme-font:minor-bidi;} FOR KANNADA Swakulasali (also known as Swakula Sali, 'Sakulasale' , Swakulasale. and Sali or Rugwedsali) is an ethnic community found among Hindus in India. They claim descent from the mythological Jihveshwara . Marathi is the primary language spoken by the Swakula Salis, who also speak local dialects. Their primary professions are weaving , designing and dyeing .&nbsp; Divine and deity Sri Jihveshwar, Shiva’s son, born through tongue of Shiva, was ordered by Jaganmata Adimaya to prepare various types of costumes by weaving and designing them for protection of body of entire gods and goddesses. &nbsp; Fostered by Jaganmata Sri Parvathi, fortunate Shri Jihveshwara being “constume designer Brahma” in Kailasa performed his day to day routine. &nbsp; After getting Brahmopadesha from Lord Brahma himself he completed his study of Vedas in order to attain spirituality and Mukti. Sri Jihveshwara is the first scholar who invented not only the art of weaving strands available in the nature such as cotton and silk with excellent concepts, fascinating amalgamation of natural colours, with expertise of creating costumes in various designs and styles, but also invented tools for preparing these costumes. &nbsp;&nbsp; He married Ankini and Dashankini, created by Brahma and Saraswathi, in the presence of all gods and goddesses and followed Grahasthashrama. &nbsp; He spread the weaving profession developed by himself through his sons throughout. &nbsp; Four sons of Ankini surrendered themselves in service of Guru and six sons of Dashankini engaged in continuing the task of their father. &nbsp; Various classes of Salis were manifested through them only and spread all over the world as Sali gene. &nbsp; Those who design and weave various types of clothes are ‘Sali Community’. &nbsp; Sons of Sri Jihvewshara gradually came down to bhuloka and were introduced as ‘Swakulasali Samaj’, preparing and distributing costumes to entire humanity. &nbsp; Swakulasalies are ‘Vastrakarma Brahmanas’ just like Vishwakarma Brahmanas, a semi-brahmin community, who are in the traditional profession of sculpture and making ornaments. &nbsp; There are various gotras of leading maharshis, who follow upanayana and upakarma rituals. &nbsp; They are yajurvedis and followers of Apastanbha principle.&nbsp; When Kailas was in danger in the absence of Lord Shiva, Sri Jihveshwara wearing weapons, charioteered Devaganas and killed evil forces. &nbsp; Renowned as good warrior Sri Jihveshwara after enjoying worldly grahastha dharma, he received sanyasa deeksha from his father, Parashiva, wore kashambara and attained spiritual target. &nbsp; He administered spirituality to his sons also. &nbsp;&nbsp; When his parents relaxed in Kashi kshetra as Vishwanath and Vishalakshi, he resided in Kashi in the presence of Shiva as Kalabhairava as per blessings of Shiva, serving him there. &nbsp; There is Sri &nbsp; Jihveshwara (Jihvaji) temple now also in Goghat, wherein daily nanda deep, bhajan and pujas are going on. &nbsp; Though Sri Parameshwara is family deity of Swakulasalis, there is no difference between Hari-Hara, the gods in mind. &nbsp; They are the worshippers of Sri Tulaja Bhavani and Sri Dattatreya too. &nbsp; Without confining to any creed, they are following Smarta rituals. &nbsp; They follow Warakari rituals of Pandharpur also very sincerely. &nbsp; They celebrate all Hindu festivals by devotion. &nbsp; The Swakulasali Community which migrated from Uttaranchal, Kashi origin has settled in Devagiri and Pratistanpur (Paithan) of Maharashtra. &nbsp; It may be observed that sarees of Paithani style are still famous. &nbsp; As they settled in Maharasthra for many centuries original language of Swakulasalis became Marathi. But Swakulasalis are not Marathas (Kshatriyas). &nbsp; When Chatrapathi Shivaji’s father Shahajirao Bhonsle and Ghorpade warriors came to south from Maharashtra for battles, Swakulasalis who accompanied them went towards south and settled in Karnataka and Andhra regions. &nbsp; Though Marathi is their mother tongue they have embraced regional languages affectionately for business and education. &nbsp; Gradually, as they stayed away from their original place and were lagging behind financially and educationally, a few of them have also mixed with Maratha Kshatriyas changing their food and formalities, without any commitment to their original culture. &nbsp; The birthday celebration of Sri Jihveshwara by the Swakulasali Samaj brethren, understanding the very gist of &nbsp; standard life, spiritual thinking, profession and sincerity to religion guided by Sri Jivehshwara and inculcating them in their life, is a meaningful celebration. &nbsp; It has become their tradition to perform special sixteen services of puja (shodashopachar), cradle function, lullaby - singing songs to make him sleep, reading Swakulasali purana, keertana-bhajana, chariot festival, distributing anna prasad, &nbsp; arranging functions for organizing the Samaj etc., spearheading religious and cultural programmes appropriately. &nbsp;&nbsp; This Swakulasali Samaj is inseparable portion of Hindu religion. &nbsp; Due to globalization, industrialization and modernization the profession of weaving clothes has come to standstill resulting in severe threat to the very existence of the Samaj. &nbsp; Suffered from all these, the Samaj has become very poor, hesitating minded backward community. &nbsp; They are so innocent that they mingle with the local people and get localized easily. &nbsp; As Swakulasalis are Shaivas, they mingle in the language and living styles of Lingayats of north Karnataka and live as Veerashaivas without any difficulties. &nbsp; A few of them because of their satvik nature and practice of vaidik formalities they seem to be brahimins. &nbsp; In Andhra Pradesh and Telangana there are people who forget their own Marathi language following their daily performance while co-living with Telugu linguist. &nbsp; In Tamil Nadu we need others assistance to recognize families of Swakulasali samaj. &nbsp; So, the scattered Swakulasalai Samaj having become lowest population, have depended on other professions going away from traditional weaving. &nbsp; In today’s competitive world, Swakulasali Samaj brethren have to remain organized to retain their existence, for thorough development of Samaj brethren, celebrate Bhagawan Sri Jihveshwar jayanthi congregating in huge number through various Swakulasali oranisations established in leading cities of the country. There are organizations at national level and state level. &nbsp; Hostel and community homes are established in Molakalmuru with the assistance of Government. &nbsp; State level educational institution, Gurupeetha and Sri Jihweshwar Mandir are also being established there with the financial assistance of donors and well-wishers of the Samaj. &nbsp; Assistance of Government is sought for construction of Swakulaslai community homes in various cities of the country. &nbsp; The Samaj is looking forward to the helping hand of the Government to get land for suitable accommodation for state level development schemes of the community in the capital. Facing many challenges in today’s competitive world, &nbsp; knowing the speed, necessity and inevitability to cover the developmental path &nbsp; by the Swakulasali Samaj in various dimensions in several fields like religious, political, educational, industrial, commercial, technical, cultural, fine arts, sports, media, communication and social service, the present youth are marching ahead towards achievement under guidance of senior generation. === TITLE:  Sri Jihveshwara ===
Bhagwan Sri Jihveshwara Except Omkar Swarop parabramha, everything on this earth&nbsp; was shunya. Thru the Supreme tejas of Parabramha “adimaya” took birth. Upon instructions from para bramha, the adimaya created Vishnu from her satvaguna and Bramha from Rajoguna,and Maheswar from Tamoguna. She entrusted the responsibility of creation to Bramha,and to ruling by Vishnu and for destruction by Maheswar. Devas and the Manvas were living and travelling nude. In the advent of the nature’s heat and cold waves the awareness of protection&nbsp; from such extremeties led to wearing of the Leaves, skin, wooden barks etc&nbsp; around them for covering their bodies. As time passed &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;there was an &nbsp;increase of natural intelligence in them, Hence weaving of cloth from the trees yarn, barks etc &nbsp;was created by them. Hence ADIMAYA requested SHIVA to create a Punya Purusha who can weave exquisite clothes. Upon instructions from ADIMAYA, SHIVA created a child form his tongue (JIHVE) in Shravan Masa, Trayodashi, early morning on Monday. The naming ceremony was performed in great pomp&nbsp; at Kailas. The boy was named SALI to assist everybody in weaving the cloth. Godess Parvati named his kula as SWAKULA for promoting in the mankind. As the child was born thru the tongue of Shiva, Godess Parvati named him as “JIHVESHWARA”. Also &nbsp;the child was born at the time of Sun rise , hence his Gotra was named as SURYAVAMSHI GOTRA. After attaining the youthood the boy intends to start the job of weaving. ADIMAYA and the TRIMOORTHIES instructs all the 33 crore devathas to bless him with all powers.&nbsp; The secrets of Siva’ Creation&nbsp; and human reproduction, Nectars of the Life&amp; death’s &nbsp;secret chambers, Serving &nbsp;of Sadhus and Divya Yogis”- These were some of&nbsp; the topics being preached by Sri Jiveshwara&nbsp; to his bhaktas. Sri JIHVESHWARA’s&nbsp; grand wedding takes place in Magha Masa ,Shuddha Dwadasi-Somawara with ANKINI created by Bramha and DASHANKINI created by Saraswathi. With the divine blessings of Shiva , Ankini gives birth to 4 children namely Lomaharshi, Chandrakanath, Kshetrapala,and Parasnatha.&nbsp; Similarly Dashankini gives birth to 6 children namely Kailasabhuvana, Sanathana, Bhaktibanuja, Parvakala, Dayasagara, and Archana nirdhara. &nbsp; Matha Ankini Devi, Sri Jihveshwar, Matha Dashankini Devi Subhanu the King of Malava &nbsp;accompanied with Kailasabhuvana to a city named Satyavathi. He settles there and starts weaving the cloth, he is named and prospered as AHERSALI Community.&nbsp; The King of Bhaglana, Chitravrutti deeply prays for Vishnu and accompanies Sanathana with him to his kingdom. He continues to weave the cloth under the name” Sanathan Sali” and promotes his community.&nbsp; Jiveshwara’s children and grand children continues to weave cloth as their profession and were known by the name” Ahersali, Bhangad Sali or Sanathan Sali” – All these Community originally belongs to SWAKULA SALI. &nbsp;Jiveshwara Swamy along with his mother Paravathi and also Shiva came to Kasi and as desired by Jihveshwara he was blessed as&nbsp; Sanyasi, He wore the orange robes and got the power of&nbsp; Bhairava. Shiva Parvati blessed him and instructed of being worshipped&nbsp; as Jiveshwara from the people and since was named as KALABHAIRAVA.&nbsp; Under the blessings of Shiva the Varunaraja constructs a beautiful temple on the banks of Ganga river. This temple is being installed with the deities as Vishshweswara and Vishalaksmi whom are the other forms of Godess&nbsp; Shiva and Parvathi.&nbsp; GOGHUT a place near Kashi, where KALABHAIRAV was receiving the daily poojas from Swakula Sali &nbsp;people.Also the temple of Bhagwan Sri Jiveshwara – the head of Swakula Sali tribe is situated here. This article and the writings on Sri Jiveshwara - &nbsp;are offered to the people of the &nbsp;Swakula Sali community.We pray to Sri Jiveshwara to bless us with good education,Peace and prosperity.
